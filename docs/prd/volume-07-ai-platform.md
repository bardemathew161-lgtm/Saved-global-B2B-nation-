# Volume 7 – AI Platform

## 1. AI Objectives
The AI layer should improve discovery, conversion, sales forecasting, and operational decision-making across the business lifecycle.

## 2. AI Modules
- AI Chatbot for business support
- Smart search and semantic business discovery
- Recommendation engine for partners and leads
- Sales forecasting model
- Marketing automation suggestions
- Document analysis and summary support

## 3. AI Workflow Use Cases
- Draft follow-up messages after lead events
- Recommend inventory or pricing actions from demand signals
- Summarize contract documents and business history
- Suggest new marketplace offerings by trend analysis
