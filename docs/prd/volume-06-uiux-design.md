# Volume 6 – UI/UX Design

## 1. Design System
- Clean, high-trust business visual language
- Strong hierarchy for operational workflows
- Consistent product cards, tabs, tables, and dashboards

## 2. Brand Guidelines
- Brand tone: professional, global, ambitious
- Primary palette: deep navy, accent blue, and green success signals
- High-contrast text for accessibility and clarity

## 3. Layout Priorities
- Mobile-first responsive layout
- Fast action paths for contact, quote, order, and payment tasks
- Dashboard-first landing experience for business managers

## 4. Key User Journeys
- Discover business and supplier
- Capture lead and convert to opportunity
- Generate invoice and complete payment
- Review analytics and AI recommendations

## 5. Accessibility
- Keyboard navigation support
- Contrast ratio compliance
- Clear focus indicators and scalable labels
